## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-24

Initial private baseline: Rails/Inertia application foundation, deterministic
development workflow, first-party closed access, Rails-native jobs/files/mail,
provider-neutral durable integration operations with a fake-provider proof and
optional OTLP export, one production image, build-time profiles and recipes, and
thin deployment recipes for Compose, exe.dev, Render, Fly.io, and a generic VPS.

This release does not imply a license, public availability, or hosted deployment
validation.
